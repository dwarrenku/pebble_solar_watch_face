## Short description

The sun's path through your day, with a cartoon sun, the weather and three complications.

## Description

Sunpath draws the sun's path for where you are. The sun sits on the curve at the current time, above the horizon by day and below it at night, with sunrise and sunset marked on the horizon.

- A cartoon sun in the style of the Pebble timeline: it peeks over the horizon at sunrise, wakes up as it climbs, and sleeps under the horizon at night while the moon comes out with its real phase.
- Five themes: Solar glow with the cartoon sun, cartoon sun on white, cartoon sun on black, plain Solar glow, and Classic.
- Current weather with the day's high and low, from the Pebble app's weather plugin or Open-Meteo.
- Three complications you choose: next sunrise or sunset, daylight left, golden hour, moon, battery, steps, distance, heart rate, rain chance, feels-like, humidity, wind, UV, phone battery, next calendar event and more.
- Six time sizes and styles, 12 or 24-hour clock, and five date formats.
- Flick your wrist for first light, sunrise, solar noon, sunset, last light and how the day's length compares with yesterday.
- A live preview in the settings page.
- The watch computes the sun and moon itself, so the face keeps working without the phone.

Needs location access to place the sun. Open source (MIT).
